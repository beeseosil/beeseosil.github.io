Seeds

It is important to use hydroponic seeds or quality seeds. General commercial seeds are sometimes not good for hydroponics. Old seeds are not recommended as the germination rate is not good. Seeds not good for hydroponics sometimes shows severe stunted growth when EC reaches 1.0+.

Seeds are usually good for germination in 15~20 celsius. 20 celsius is generally good. Below 15 and above 25 is harmful for germination and growth.

When using rockwool, you don't need to soak the seeds. Just place the seeds on a damp rockwool and cover with a small amount of rockwool or sponge debris.

It's important to use distilled or purified water at the germination stage. This is important for the early stage of growth. After the emergence of a radicle, you can use tap water or nutrient water with a very low EC (<0.6). High EC or TDS (total dissolved solids) is detrimental to a sprout.

Germination time is usually 2~3 days in a dark place. The sprouts can be seen after 2~3 days. When the seed emerges a small radicle, transfer the seeding sponges with a radicle or sprout to a bright space. Sacrifice the most recent or smallest, damaged sprouts.

Seedling quality is critical. A epigenetic variety of crop plant seeds is not so many. A weak sprout or seedling is outstandingly stunted than normal ones, and never catch up the shape. Don't hesitate to sacrifice those. Or you may have those as a back-up.

Transplant the square sponge to a pot sponge when the sprout has a true leaf more than 1. Do not pull off the sporuts from seeding sponges. The square sponge should go with the sprout. It's good to transplant as soon as possible if the sprout has a true leaf with a propagated root.

Any seedlings or sporuts not in the container cannot be neglected. Sprouts easily dies when dry. Roots can be entangled. Watching sprouts with such a condition takes a time and it's good to have the same time point of germination.
