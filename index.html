Why

I saw a writing about an aeroponic potato in Nasa by accident. I was thinking of growing small plants, but I decided to raising hydroponic plants because the photo of professor Kratky (known by a deep culture method with his name) with practical articles.

Hydroponics at a Room

Pros

Possible to control or offer environmental parameters: the vital things are light (daily total light amount, or DLI) and nutrient water. An automation is possible for picky plants.

Higher plant density: thanks to the larger available root environment.

Larger or heavier yield or shorter crop cycle.

Less unfavorable insects, free from diseases.

Rooms for many experiments and milestones.

Cons

To achieve the better and faster growth, an artificial light source is must. It's questionable that doing hydroponics without a powerful light.

Have to calculate or buy something.

Crop Plants

Edible plants are distinct from pot plants. I have seen of not only analysis articles but also raising manuals always for higher numerical outputs.

There are established strict procedures for those plants for better yield and quality products. The market standard for a plant crop seems to be extremely high.

While get interested, I found such processes for each plant has outstanding amount of labor, technique, and knowledege.

Crop plants are also lovely, and has distinct smell.
