I saw a writing about an aeroponic potato in Nasa by accident. I was thinking of growing small plants, but I decided to raising hydroponic plants because the photo of professor Kratky (known by a deep culture method with his name) with a practical article for anyone not involved in horticulture.

Edible Plants

Edible plants are distinct from pot plants. I have seen of not only analysis articles but also raising manuals always for higher outputs.

There are established procedures for those plants for better yield and quality products. The market standard for a plant crop seems to be extremely high. Such processes for each plant has outstanding amount of labor, technique, and knowledege. This gives a recognizable objective for growing plants.

Hydroponics advantages

  • Possible to control environmental parameters 환경 통제 가능
  • Larger yield, crop cycle manipulation 작물 사이클을 다룰 수 있음
  • Higher plant density thanks to the larger root volume and aeration 재식 밀도 증가
  • Disease-free, less unfavorable insects 병충해가 적음
  • Room for experiments

Hydroponics problems

  • Artificial light is tramendously energy inefficient especially for non leaf-greens 완전한 인공광 사용의 비효율성
  • Failing to harmonize a soil-like results a missed crop cycle or fatal event 실수가 무마되지 않고 중대한 생리 문제가 발생
  • Untamed labor for specific hydroponics practices 수경재배 자체 노동이 있음
  • Demands consequential self-oriented technical applications to resolve the above 단일분야 문제 해결에 다수분야 이용 필요
