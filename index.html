계기

I saw an Wiki article about an aeroponic potato in Nasa by an accident. I was thinking of growing small plants, but I decided to try hydroponics leafy green because the photo of professor Kratky and his practical articles.

Why

The price of vegetable is expensive, and most people are relying on a grocery. A common vegetable plant is a crop plant with an established procedure for such a plant for quality and quantity. The market standard for a plant crop seems to be strict.

While getting interested, I found such per-plant procedures containing outstanding amount of technique, knowledege, and labor.

Also crop plants are cute, and has distinct smell since the sprouts.

Hydroponics advantages

Possible to control environmental parameters

Higher plant density thanks to the larger available root volume and air, strain segmentation

Larger yield, crop cycle manipulation

Less unfavorable insects and disease-free

Rooms for experiments

Hydroponics problems

Harmonizing a soil-like matrix: a missed crop cycle or fatal event happen easily

Have to buy something unusual in conventional gardening

More labor for some practices

To achieve better and faster growth, an artificial light source is must, and that is extremely energy inefficient in summer because high temperature is harmful to flowers
